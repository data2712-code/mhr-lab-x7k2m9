TD-11 · BP01-054 「Ultron-Spawn Rebuild」 Ultron

  • EN: "…you move 1 of your characters with Lv4 or above and 【Ultron】 in their features from RETREAT back to your HAND."
  • ID: "…kembalikan 1 karakter Lv4 atau lebih tinggi bernama [Ultron] dari RETREAT ke tangan Anda."
  • No card has the feature 【Ultron】 (Ultron cards are 【Machine】), so the English wording cannot be applied as printed. This is an exception to the usual "follow the English" rule: return 1 Lv4 or higher character with Ultron in its name, as the Indonesian text says and as the other BP01 Ultron cards (BP01-014, BP01-037, BP01-043) are written.
  • Reading decided by MHR Deck Lab (6 Nov 2026), not an official erratum.
