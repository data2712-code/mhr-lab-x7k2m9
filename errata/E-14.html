Errata & Text Differences · Errata V.2 (October 2026)

E-14 · SP01-061 「Spider-Archenemy」 Mr. Negative — Indonesian

Change: compare your set cards in BASE with your Characters in BASE, not in BATTLE.

  • ID before: "…jumlah kartu tertutup di BASE sama dengan jumlah karakter di BATTLE Anda…" → after: "…jumlah karakter di BASE Anda…"
  • The English text already said "your characters in BASE".

Ruling: "flip" follows the English text (confirmed by the Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia).

  • EN: "you flip all your cards in BASE". ID: "ubah semua kartu di BASE Anda menjadi terbuka", which reads as if only Face-Down Cards are turned face-up.
  • Correct play: every card in your BASE is flipped. Your Characters in BASE are turned face-down first, then your Face-Down Cards are flipped face-up (CR 301.2.a.2).
  • Example: your BASE has 2 Characters (A and B) and 2 Face-Down Cards (X and Y). After Mr. Negative, X and Y are face-up Characters and A and B are Face-Down Cards. You do not end up with 4 face-up Characters.
  • A and B lose their effects and are no longer Characters, so any Attached Cards under them go to the RETREAT (CR 301.25.l). X and Y did not enter the field, but they can still use BATTLE-BASE MOVE this turn (see Rules FAQ F-11).
