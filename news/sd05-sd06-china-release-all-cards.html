SD05 Soul & SD06 Power Launch in China on October 16: All 58 Cards Translated

16 Oktober 2026 · 22 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

Orange and Purple arrive in China on October 16 with Starter Decks SD05 Soul and SD06 Power, plus the PB02 promo pack. Every SD05, SD06 and PB02 card, fan-translated into English.

Starter Deck SD05 "Soul" (Orange) and Starter Deck SD06 "Power" (Purple) release in China on October 16, 2026, bringing two new colours to Marvel Hero Rush. Every box also includes the PB02 promo pack with 21 PR cards. With the last spoilers in, all 36 starter deck card numbers are now known, so here is the complete list: 18 Orange cards, 18 Purple cards (plus an SEC version of Thanos) and all 21 PB02 promos.

All card text in this article is a fan translation from the Chinese cards, written in the style of official English card text and the terminology of Comprehensive Rules 1.03. Card names, trait names and wording may differ once the official English and Indonesian versions are released. An Indonesian release date has not been confirmed yet.

At a Glance

  • SD05 Soul (Orange): 1 MR, 2 GR, 3 SR and 12 R. Asgard (Odin, Hela, Heimdall, Sif, Valkyrie and New Valkyrie), Kamar-Taj (Ancient One, Wong and three Doctor Stranges) and Captain Marvel.
  • SD06 Power (Purple): 1 UR (plus an SEC alternate art), 2 GR, 3 SR and 12 R. Thanos and the Black Order, plus the Cabal: Chitauri, Silver Surfer, Ronan and Adam Warlock.
  • PB02 Promo Pack: 21 PR cards, 10 Orange, 10 Purple and 1 Yellow.
  • Two new keywords not yet covered in Comprehensive Rules 1.03: RAMPAGE* on Thanos and QUICK STRIKE* on Ancient One. Both names are provisional translations.

SD05 Soul: Orange

Orange is built around discarding and bringing characters back. Many cards discard the top card of your deck or a card from your HAND, then return a Human or Asgard character from RETREAT. Odin pressures your opponent's BASE from the BACK position, Captain Marvel can prune herself together with an opposing character, and Ancient One revives an Orange character whenever your opponent plays one.

SD06 Power: Purple

Purple feeds on its own RETREAT and VOID. Most Black Order cards prune a purple or Cabal character from RETREAT when they are called, and several get a bigger effect while they sit in FRONT: Ebony Maw's Power reduction grows from 1000 to 3000, Black Dwarf's R reduction from 1 to 3, and Proxima Midnight and Corvus Glaive can retreat opposing characters outright. Cabal cards like Silver Surfer, Ronan and Chitauri Chariot then trigger when they enter VOID from RETREAT by your effect.
