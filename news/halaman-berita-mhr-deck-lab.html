News Page Launches on MHR Deck Lab

26 September 2026 · 1 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

Marvel Hero Rush news, cards you can click straight from the article, clean WhatsApp previews, and RSS.

MHR Deck Lab now has a News page. Here we round up Marvel Hero Rush news: set releases, card spoilers, rule changes, tournament results, and LGS events.

Features

  • Clickable cards. Card names in an article, like SP01-021 [The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man, open that card's detail straight away.
  • Clean WhatsApp previews. The Share button uses a dedicated link that shows the article's title and image.
  • RSS. Use the RSS link on the News page to subscribe via an RSS reader or a Discord/Telegram bot.

Got LGS event info or tournament results from your city? Send them to us via TikTok @deteprtm.
