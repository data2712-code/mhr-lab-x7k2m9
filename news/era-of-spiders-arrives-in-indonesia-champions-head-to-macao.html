Era of Spiders Arrives in Indonesia as Four Champions Head to Macao

9 Oktober 2026 · 2 menit baca · oleh MHR Deck Lab Team

Era of Spiders (SP01) launched in Indonesia on October 3, and four qualifier winners are flying to Macao for the Asia Peak Invitational at ASCC on October 11.

Era of Spiders (SP01) is here

The second expansion for Marvel Hero Rush, and its first special expansion, launched in Indonesia on October 3, 2026. The set is built around the Spider-Man Universe, with Spider-Man variants from across the multiverse plus their allies and villains. Unlike BP01: The Avengers, SP01 has all-foil cards, and it introduces a new rarity, HR, in gold and silver marks. The set contains 80 Character Cards, 20 Rush Point Cards and 50 Alternative Art Cards.

Browse the set and build

Open the SP01 set page to look through the cards, then try Spider-heavy builds in the deck builder, like the Red-Blue lists that won Jakarta, Bandung and Surabaya.

The Champions go to ASCC

Four players won the Indonesian qualifiers on October 4 and are now heading to Macao: Radian (Bandung), Edwin (Jakarta), Yogie (Batam) and Luthfi (Surabaya).

They will play in the Marvel Hero Rush Asia Peak Invitational on October 11, held as part of the 4th Asia Super Collectors Convention (ASCC) at The Venetian Macao Cotai Expo Hall D&E. Cardfun will be at Booth A12. Read their qualifier coverage: Jakarta and Batam, Bandung and Surabaya.
