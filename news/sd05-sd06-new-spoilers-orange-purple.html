16 More Orange & Purple Cards Revealed: Hela, Silver Surfer, Ronan and More

9 Oktober 2026 · 6 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

Sixteen new Rares from Starter Decks SD05 (Orange) and SD06 (Purple) have been revealed: three Helas, Heimdall, Sif, Silver Surfer, Ronan, Adam Warlock and the Chitauri. Fan-translated card details inside.

Sixteen more cards from the Orange and Purple starter decks have been revealed ahead of the China release on 16 October 2026. All sixteen are Rares (R), split evenly between SD05 (Orange, "Soul") and SD06 (Purple, "Power"). They follow the key cards we covered in our earlier SD05, SD06 and PB02 preview.

All card text below is a fan translation from the Chinese spoiler images, written in the style of official English card text and the terminology of Comprehensive Rules 1.03. Names, trait translations and wording may differ once the cards are released outside China.

Orange (SD05 Soul): Hela and the Asgardians

Hela «Execution of the Nine Realms» (SD05-016) is the Orange headliner: Lv6, R-1, Power 4000, trait Niflheim. When she enters the field you may discard the top card of your deck, and if you do, you attach 1 of your Asgard characters from RETREAT to her. In your turn, while she has exactly 1 attached card, she gets Power+2000, which makes her a 6000-Power attacker with R-1.

The other two Helas are both Lv6 and work from RETREAT. Each one triggers when it enters RETREAT by the discarding of an effect:

  • Hela «Jailbreak Rebirth» (SD05-017), R-2, Power 3000: you may place 1 of your characters with Hela in its name from RETREAT into your field.
  • Hela «Summoning of the Dead» (SD05-018), R-3, Power 2500: you may place 1 of your Asgard characters from RETREAT into your field.

Put together with the top-card discards on the other Orange cards, this gives Orange a plan: discard on purpose, then bring the right character back.

  • Heimdall «Rainbow Light of Exile» (SD05-014), Lv4, R-1, Power 4000, Asgard. ACTI【BATTLE/ONCE PER TURN】: discard 1 card from your HAND, then move 1 opponent's character in BATTLE with Power equal to or lower than Heimdall's Power to its BASE.
  • Sif «Judgment of Exile» (SD05-010), Lv3, R-1, Power 3500, Asgard / Avengers. When this card enters the field, you may discard the top card of your deck. If you do, move 1 opponent's Lv3 or lower character from BATTLE to its BASE.
  • Sif «Judgment of Broken Wings» (SD05-011), Lv2, R-0, Power 2000, Asgard / Avengers. When this card enters the field, you may discard 1 card from your HAND. If you do, move 1 opponent's character from WING to its BASE.
  • Sif «Shield of Asgard» (SD05-012), Lv3, R-0, Power 6000, Asgard / Avengers. This card can not be called. ACTI【HAND】: show this card, then move it from HAND back to the top of your deck.
  • New Valkyrie «Blessing of the Einherjar» (SD05-013), Lv3, R-1, Power 2000, Asgard. ACTI【BATTLE/ONCE PER TURN】: discard 1 card from your HAND. If you do, 1 of your characters in the field gets R+1 and Power+1000 this turn, and an extra Power+1000 if it has Asgard in its features.

Purple (SD06 Power): Cabal, Chitauri and the VOID

Purple's new Rares lean on a different zone: VOID. Silver Surfer «Energy Reversal» (SD06-015), Lv3, R-2, Power 2000, Zenn-La / Cabal, triggers when it enters VOID from RETREAT by your effect. 1 of your opponent's characters in BATTLE gets Power-1000 this turn, and if you do, the Surfer moves from VOID back to the bottom of your deck, so he keeps coming back.

  • Ronan «Starship Command» (SD06-016), Lv3, R-1, Power 3500, Kree Empire / Cabal. When this card enters VOID from RETREAT by your effect, you may place 1 of your Lv2 or lower Cabal characters from VOID into your BATTLE.
  • Adam Warlock «Quest for Allies» (SD06-017), Lv2, R-2, Power 2000, Cabal. When this card enters the field, you may discard the top card of your deck. If you do, you may move 1 of your Cabal characters from RETREAT back to your HAND.
  • Adam Warlock «Unsealed Magic Burst» (SD06-018), Lv5, R-1, Power 4000, Cabal. When this card enters the field, you may discard the top card of your deck. If you do, retreat 1 opponent's character in BATTLE with Lv3 or below and ACTI in its effects.

The Chitauri add four Lv1-2 bodies, all with the Chitauri and Cabal traits:

  • Chitauri Soldier «Swarm Assault» (SD06-011), Lv2, R-2, Power 2000. When this card enters the field, you may discard the top card of your deck. If you do, 1 of your opponent's characters in BATTLE gets Power-1000 this turn.
  • Leviathan «Colossal Palm Slam» (SD06-012), Lv2, R-1, Power 3000. When this card enters the field, you may discard the top card of your deck. If you do, move 1 opponent's Lv3 or lower character from BATTLE to its BASE.
  • War Envoy «Interstellar Crossing» (SD06-013), Lv1, R-0, Power 1500. In your opponent's turn, all of your Lv4 Black Order characters in HAND get the ability COUNTER. While in BASE, your characters in FRONT get R+1.
  • Chitauri Chariot «Costly Reinforcement» (SD06-014), Lv2, R-3, Power 2000. When this card enters VOID from RETREAT by your effect, you draw 1 card and discard 1 card from your HAND.

What stands out

  • Sending characters back to BASE is all over Orange and Purple: Heimdall, two Sifs and Leviathan all move opposing characters there.
  • Top-card discards feed RETREAT and VOID. Both colours have cards that only pay off once something has been discarded or pruned, so expect these decks to cycle their own graveyards.
  • War Envoy supports the new Black Order trait, giving your Lv4 Black Order characters in HAND COUNTER on the opponent's turn.
