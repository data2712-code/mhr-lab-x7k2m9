Regionals 2026 Indonesia: Yogie Wins with Red-Green, Guardians of the Galaxy Reach the Final

23 November 2026 · 4 menit baca · oleh MHR Deck Lab Team

Yogie's Red-Green deck won Regionals 2026 at AEON Mall BSD. Axel finished 2nd with an all-new Orange-Purple Guardians list. Full Top 8 standings and all eight decklists inside.

Yogie won Marvel Hero Rush Regionals 2026 at AEON Mall BSD with a Red-Green deck. Axel finished 2nd with an Orange-Purple list built entirely from the newest releases. Edwin (Red-Yellow) and Radian (Red-Green) rounded out the Top 4.

All eight decklists are already on the Tournament Decks page. Open any entry and click "Load into Deck Builder" to see the full 50-card list.

Final Standings

  • 1st Place — Yogie, Red / Green — open deck
  • 2nd Place — Axel, Orange / Purple — open deck
  • Top 4 — Edwin, Red / Yellow — open deck
  • Top 4 — Radian, Red / Green — open deck
  • Top 8 — Karl, Yellow / Green — open deck
  • Top 8 — Budi, Red / Orange — open deck
  • Top 8 — Rama, Red / Blue — open deck
  • Top 8 — Darwin, Red / Blue — open deck

Red in 6 of 8 Decks

[Covert Ops] Black Widow[Antimatter] Iron Man

Six of the eight Top 8 decks played Red, down from seven at the October Multiverse Battle. All six ran three copies of BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow and at least two of BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man. Only Axel's Orange-Purple and Karl's Yellow-Green lists played no Red at all.

Era of Spiders is everywhere: seven of the eight decks include SP01 cards. The one exception is the runner-up.

The Champion: Red-Green

[God of Trick] Loki[Spider-Companion] Black Cat[Antimatter] Iron Man[Thunder Speed] Thor

Yogie's deck is 21 Red and 29 Green, and 20 of its 50 cards come from SP01. The Red side is built on 3× BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man, 3× BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow, 3× BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor, 3× SP01-006 [Spider-Archenemy] Red Goblin and 3× SP01-007 [Spider-Companion] Spinneret. The Green side runs full playsets of SD04-001 [God of Trick] Loki, SD04-010 [Coiled Strike] Hydra, BP01-095 [Sacrifice For Justice] Captain America, BP01-100 [Hearing Disarm] Daredevil, BP01-106 [Search For Comrade] Falcon, SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-071 [Spider-Archenemy] Venom 2099 and SP01-080 [Spider-Archenemy] Shocker.

Radian's Top 4 list uses the same Red-Green core but spreads it across 25 different cards. He also added SD04-002 [Clustered Arrow] Hawkeye, SD04-009 [Tactical Repositioning] Captain America, BP01-021 [Thunder Caller] Thor and SD01-011 [The Retaliator] Black Panther.

Axel: Guardians of the Galaxy in the Final

[Genius Burden] Thanos[Count Me In] Gamora[Rooted Revival] Groot[Ace Rescue] The Milano

Every card in Axel's 26 Orange / 24 Purple deck comes from the latest releases: 33 from PB02, 9 from SD05 and 8 from SD06. The Orange side is the Guardians: 3× PB02-001 [Rooted Revival] Groot, 3× PB02-003 [Ace Rescue] The Milano, 3× PB02-004 [Count Me In] Gamora, 3× PB02-008 [Life-and-Death Bond] Rocket Raccoon and 3× PB02-009 [Soul-Shattering Assault] Drax, plus SD05-002 [Guardian of Cosmos] Captain Marvel, SD05-011 [Judgment for Exile] Sif and SD05-017 [Reborn from Hell] Hela. The Purple side pairs Captain Marvel (PB02-012 [Photon Blast] Captain Marvel, PB02-013 [Binary Burst] Captain Marvel, PB02-016 [Power Barrage] Captain Marvel) with Thanos: SD06-001 [Genius Burden] Thanos, PB02-011 [Power of Stones] Thanos and PB02-017 [Iron-Fisted Rule] Thanos.

Budi (Top 8) played the same Guardians package in Red-Orange, with 36 Orange cards and Red support from BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow, BP01-015 [Disintegration Ray] Vision and BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man.

Edwin: Ultron and Hulkbuster

[Genius Burden] Ultron[Ultron Army] Ultron[MK44-The Brawler] Hulk Buster[Hero File] Thor

Edwin's 24 Red / 26 Yellow list was the only Red-Yellow deck in the Top 8. The Yellow side plays 3× BP01-031 [Genius Burden] Ultron, 3× BP01-037 [Ultron Army] Ultron, 3× BP01-040 [Death And Rebirth] Ultron and 3× SD02-005 [The Drone Surge] Ultron for Ultron, plus BP01-035 [MK44-The Commander] Hulk Buster, SD02-012 [Hero File] Thor and SD02-013 [Hero File] Vision. The Red side adds 3× BP01-023 [MK44-The Brawler] Hulk Buster Hulkbuster to the usual BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man and BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow.

The Rest of the Top 8

  • Karl (Yellow-Green): 44 of 50 cards from SP01, with the full Yellow Web package (SP01-021 [The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man, SP01-022 [Phantom Strike] Ghost-Spider, SP01-025 [Gunfire Negotiation] Spider-Man Noir, SP01-026 [Vigil of Love] Amazing Spider-Man, SP01-027 [Symbiotic Suit] Spider-Man, SP01-030 [Invitation of Multiverse] Spider-Ham, SP01-032 [Alliance Leader] Spider-Man 2099) and 3× PB02-021 [Fleeting Illusion] Mary Jane Watson.
  • Rama and Darwin (Red-Blue): both 12 Red / 38 Blue, built on Captain America and Knull: BP01-077 [I Need You] Captain America, SD03-011 [Ancestral Plane] New Black Panther, SP01-047 [God of Symbiotes] Knull, SP01-051 [Spider-Archenemy] Agony and SP01-054 [Spider-Archenemy] Lasher.

Side Event: 3v3 Team Battle

The 3v3 team side event at Regionals 2026 was won by Fajar, Rafid and Ferdy. The three of them covered five different colours between them:

  • Fajar: Green / Yellow
  • Rafid: Red / Blue
  • Ferdy: Orange / Green
