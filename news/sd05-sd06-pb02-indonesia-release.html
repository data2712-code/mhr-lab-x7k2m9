SD05, SD06 and PB02 Are Out in Indonesia: Official English Text Now on MHR Deck Lab

14 November 2026 · 3 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

Starter Decks SD05 Soul and SD06 Power plus the PB02 promo pack release today, 14 November 2026. All 57 cards now use official English text and are tournament-legal.

Starter Deck SD05 "Soul" (Orange), Starter Deck SD06 "Power" (Purple) and the PB02 promo pack are officially released in Indonesia today, 14 November 2026. All 57 cards are now on MHR Deck Lab with their official English names, text and card images, and they are tournament-legal in Events from today.

At a Glance

  • SD05 Soul (Orange): 18 cards, with Odin in UR, MR and SEC versions. Asgard (Odin, Hela, Heimdall, Sif, Valkyrie and New Valkyrie), Kamar-Taj (Ancient One, Wong and Doctor Strange) and Captain Marvel.
  • SD06 Power (Purple): 18 cards, with Thanos in UR, MR and SEC versions. Thanos, the Black Order and The Cabal (Chitauri, Ronan, Silver Surfer and Adam Warlock).
  • PB02 Promo Pack: 21 PR cards (10 Orange, 10 Purple, 1 Yellow). PB02 is not sold separately: it only comes with a purchase of SD05 or SD06.
  • Two new keywords: QUICK ATTACK and ONSLAUGHT.

SD05 Soul: Orange

[Guardian of Nine Realms] Odin[Guardian of Cosmos] Captain Marvel[Guardian of Earth] Ancient One[Nine Realms Executioner] Hela

Orange puts pressure on your opponent from the back row. SD05-001 [Guardian of Nine Realms] Odin lowers the Power of one of your opponent's characters in BASE at the end of your turn, and draws you an extra card if that character is retreated. SD05-002 [Guardian of Cosmos] Captain Marvel can prune itself together with an opposing character, and SD05-003 [Guardian of Earth] Ancient One places an Orange character back onto BATTLE when your opponent's character enters the field.

SD06 Power: Purple

[Genius Burden] Thanos[Peerless Blade] Corvus Glaive[Peerless Spear] Proxima Midnight[Arcane Explosion] Adam Warlock

Purple prunes its own characters in RETREAT to power up its Black Order. SD06-001 [Genius Burden] Thanos retreats a Lv4-or-lower character when called, and Lv4 Black Order cards such as SD06-002 [Peerless Blade] Corvus Glaive and SD06-003 [Peerless Spear] Proxima Midnight trigger extra effects from FRONT. The Cabal cards return each other from RETREAT and VOID.

PB02 Promo Pack

[Rooted Revival] Groot[Sweet and Gentle] Chewie[Soul-Shattering Assault] Drax[Power of Stones] Thanos[Soul Resonance] Yondu[Fleeting Illusion] Mary Jane Watson

PB02 adds Guardians of the Galaxy cards in Orange, Thanos, Captain Marvel and Yondu in Purple, and one Yellow card, PB02-021 [Fleeting Illusion] Mary Jane Watson.

New Keywords

  • QUICK ATTACK (SD05-003 [Guardian of Earth] Ancient One): AUTO【BATTLE】: In your turn, this card is not going to be retreated by Both Lose.
  • ONSLAUGHT (SD06-001 [Genius Burden] Thanos): AUTO【BATTLE】: If this card attacks the weakness successfully, you place 1 more Rush Point card.

What Changed from the Fan Translation

Our earlier article used fan translations from the Chinese cards. These have now been replaced with the official English text. The main changes:

  • RAMPAGE is officially ONSLAUGHT, and QUICK STRIKE is officially QUICK ATTACK.
  • The trait Cabal is officially The Cabal.
  • Many card titles changed. For example, SD06-001 is now 「Genius Burden」Thanos, PB02-001 is 「Rooted Revival」Groot, and PB02-002 is 「Sweet and Gentle」Chewie (previously translated as Goose).

The Indonesian card text on MHR Deck Lab is still our own translation until the official Indonesian text is published.
