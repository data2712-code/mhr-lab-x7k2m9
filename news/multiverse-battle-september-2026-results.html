Multiverse Battle September 2026: Red Ran Every Top 8 Deck

27 September 2026 · 2 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

Adzuf's Red-Green list took the trophy at Gramedia World BSD — but the bigger story is that all 8 Top-cut decks played Red. Full standings and decklists inside.

Eight players fought through Swiss and the Top-cut at Gramedia World BSD for Marvel Hero Rush's latest official event. All eight decklists are already loaded into the Tournaments Deck page — open any entry and click "Load into Deck Builder" to see the full 50-card list for yourself.

  2. 2nd Place — Zul (Red / Blue)
  3. Top 4 — Geryu Geraldo (Red / Blue)
  4. Top 4 — Adam (Red / Blue)

  6. Top 8 — Karl (Red / Blue)
  7. Top 8 — Gabriel (Red / Blue)
  8. Top 8 — Setia Budi (Red / Blue)

Red Was in Every Single Top 8 Deck

Not one Top-cut player skipped Red this time — all 8 decks ran it as a primary or heavily-splashed colour. Six paired it with Blue, and two (including the eventual champion) paired it with Green. If you're building for the next local event, Red looks like the safest foundation right now.

The Championship Core

All four confirmed Top-4 decklists — regardless of which second colour they played — shared the same core:

[Antimatter] Iron Man[Covert Ops] Black Widow[Thunder Speed] Thor[Top Agent] Black Widow
  • BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man — a Lv6 SEC bomb that prunes an opposing character straight off a call.
  • BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow — cheap, repeatable Power disruption from HAND.
  • BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor — refills your hand and grants an extra attack the moment it's called.
  • BP01-022 [Top Agent] Black Widow — a 1-cost tempo play that can retreat a small blocker when your Avengers are out.
