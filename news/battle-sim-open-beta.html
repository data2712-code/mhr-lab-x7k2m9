Battle Sim is now in open beta: play Marvel Hero Rush in your browser

12 November 2026 · 3 menit baca · oleh MHR Deck Lab Team

Practice vs a bot, find a match or invite a friend online. Every rule and card effect is handled for you. Beta runs 11–18 November 2026.

Battle Sim is now open to everyone with an MHR Deck Lab account. It lets you play full games of Marvel Hero Rush in your browser, on your phone or your computer. Every rule and card effect is handled for you, so you can focus on your plays. The beta runs from 11 to 18 November 2026. Log in, open the Battle Sim tab and press PLAY.

Four ways to play

  • Practice vs Bot: play against a bot at Easy, Normal or Hard level. Undo is always allowed in practice, so you can try a different line.
  • Find a match: join the queue and get paired with another player on the Standard clock. If nobody is available, you can play a bot instead. It is clearly marked as a bot.
  • Friend online: make a private room and send the link to a friend. Pick the clock that suits you: Standard, Relaxed, Long or a 30-minute Match clock.
  • Same device: pass-and-play on one screen.

There is also a Sandbox. Set up any board position for both players and play it out with every card visible. It's handy for checking a ruling or testing a combo.

Rules on autopilot

Battle Sim follows the official comprehensive rules. CALL limits, BATTLE, the Counter Step, Rush Points, card effects and end-of-turn steps all run automatically.

  • Every released set is in: SD01–SD04, BP01, PB01, EB01, TB01 and SP01.
  • SD05 and SD06 are playable with their official English text. PB02 is playable too; its effects follow the pre-release text until the official text comes out.
  • Online games use a turn clock (2:00 per turn and 0:30 per response on Standard). A third timeout loses the game.
  • Undo online needs your opponent's permission, up to 3 times per game.
  • If your connection drops, your seat is kept for 2 minutes.
  • The Counter prompts setting (On / Auto / Off) lets you skip Counter windows you don't need.

Made for your phone

Most of you visit MHR Deck Lab on your phone, so Battle Sim was designed for phones first:

  • the whole board fits on one screen;
  • a clear "Your turn" banner shows when it's your move;
  • short hints tell you what you can do right now;
  • face-down cards are easy to spot.

The interface is available in English and Bahasa Indonesia. Card text in the simulator is in English, the same language as the official rulings.

Use your own decks

The deck menu shows the decks saved to your MHR Deck Lab account. You can also paste any deck link or deck code. Build a deck in the Deck Builder, save it, and it's ready to play.

History, replays and Performance

  • Match history keeps every finished game. Watch any game again move by move, or share the replay link with a friend.
  • Online results go to Performance automatically, in their own Battle Sim group. They are kept separate from the tabletop matches you log yourself, so your real-life stats stay clean.

Help us during the beta

This is a beta, so you may still find a bug or a card that doesn't behave the way it should. If something looks wrong or confusing, tap Report a bug or Send feedback inside Battle Sim. Your report goes straight to us, together with the game details we need to fix it.
