Macau Apex Championship Qualifiers: Yogie, Radian and Luthfi Take Batam, Bandung and Surabaya

5 Oktober 2026 · 3 menit baca

Yogie won Batam with Red-Green, while Radian and Luthfi won Bandung and Surabaya with Spider-heavy Red-Blue builds. Top 4 standings and decklist breakdowns from all three Qualifiers.

Three more Qualifiers for the Macau Apex Championship were played on October 4, 2026: Batam at Grand Batam Mall, Bandung at Paris Van Java and Surabaya at Ciputra World. All 12 Top 4 decklists are already loaded into the Tournament Decks page — open any entry and click "Load into Deck Builder" to see the full 50-card list. (The Jakarta Qualifier was played the same day: read the Jakarta results here.)

Red Made 11 of 12 Top 4 Lists

Only one finalist skipped Red: Steven Lim's Blue-Green list, the Batam runner-up. Everyone else played it. Red-Blue was the most common pairing with 7 lists (2 in Batam, 2 in Bandung, 3 in Surabaya), followed by Red-Green with 3. Edwin's Red-Yellow list in Bandung was the only one to touch Yellow.

Add the Jakarta Qualifier, where all four finalists ran Red-Blue, and Red-Blue now holds 11 of the 16 Top 4 seats across the four Qualifiers.

The Spider Surge in Bandung and Surabaya

The biggest story outside the Avengers shell is how much the Spider-Archenemy cards from SP01 showed up. Five of the 12 lists ran 26 or more SP01 cards: Radian (40), Luthfi (36), Leo of Surabaya (36), Wilson (32) and Steven Lim (26). That includes both the Bandung and Surabaya champions, and three of the four Surabaya finalists.

All five of those lists ran the same five cards as a full playset of 3: SP01-047 [God of Symbiotes] Knull, SP01-050 [Spider-Archenemy] Phage, SP01-051 [Spider-Archenemy] Agony, SP01-053 [Spider-Archenemy] Riot and SP01-054 [Spider-Archenemy] Lasher. All four Red-Blue versions also ran SP01-042 [Spider-Archenemy] Green Goblin, SP01-043 [Spider-Archenemy] Kingpin and SP01-058 [Spider-Archenemy] Tombstone.

SP01-047 [God of Symbiotes] Knull is the centrepiece. It gets R+X and Power+X000, where X is the number of cards attached to it, and it can be placed from HAND onto your BATTLE when you have 4 or more Symbiote characters in RETREAT.

The Most-Played Cards

Across all 12 lists, the same Avengers staples kept appearing:

[Covert Ops] Black Widow[Thunder Speed] Thor[Spider-Archenemy] Green Goblin[I Need You] Captain America[Antimatter] Iron Man
  • BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow — in 11 of 12 lists, and always as a full 3 copies.
  • BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor — in 10 of 12 lists, 29 copies in total.
  • SP01-042 [Spider-Archenemy] Green Goblin, BP01-077 [I Need You] Captain America and BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man — 7 lists each. BP01-015 [Disintegration Ray] Vision also made 7 lists, with fewer copies overall.

Three Champion Builds

  • Yogie (Batam, Red / Green) — the only champion without Blue: 18 Red and 32 Green. Full playsets of SD04-001 [God of Trick] Loki, BP01-100 [Hearing Disarm] Daredevil, BP01-106 [Search For Comrade] Falcon, SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-071 [Spider-Archenemy] Venom 2099 and BP01-095 [Sacrifice For Justice] Captain America, with PB01-006 [I Am Groot] Groot as a 2-of.
  • Radian (Bandung, Red / Blue) — 17 Red and 33 Blue, with 40 SP01 cards. SP01-001 [Spider-Archenemy] Carnage is the Red finisher at 3 copies, with a single copy of SP01-009 [Spider-Ally] Black Panther on the Red side.
  • Luthfi (Surabaya, Red / Blue) — the bluest list of all 12 at 36 Blue and 14 Red, with 36 SP01 cards. Beyond the Spider core it runs BP01-077 [I Need You] Captain America at 3 copies, plus single copies of PB01-003 [Galaxy Dancer] Star-Lord and BP01-061 [Follow Shadow] New Black Panther.
