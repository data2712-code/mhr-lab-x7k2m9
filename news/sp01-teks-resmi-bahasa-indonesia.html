SP01 "Era of Spiders" Now Uses Official Indonesian Text & Art

26 September 2026 · 1 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

79 SP01 cards now use official Indonesian effect text, and 114 of its images have been swapped for the Indonesian print versions.

SP01 cards on MHR Deck Lab now use official Indonesian effect text — read straight from the official print, no longer a fan translation. The card art has also been swapped to the Indonesian print version.

What Changed

  • 79 SP01 cards now use official Indonesian effect text.
  • 114 SP01 images in Indonesian-language mode are now the Indonesian print version, including MR, SEC, and HR variants.
  • The "fan translation" badge now only appears on cards whose text isn't official yet.
