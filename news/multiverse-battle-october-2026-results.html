Multiverse Battle October 2026: Yellow-Green Spiders Win, the Only Top 8 Deck Without Red

1 November 2026 · 4 menit baca · oleh MHR Deck Lab Team

Gabriel's Yellow-Green Spider deck won the Multiverse Battle at Gramedia Matraman on October 31, the only Top 8 list without Red. September champion Adzuf finished 2nd. Full standings and all eight decklists inside.

Gabriel won the Multiverse Battle October 2026 at Gramedia Matraman, Jakarta on October 31 with a Yellow-Green Era of Spiders deck. It was the only deck in the Top 8 that didn't play Red. September champion Adzuf reached the final again and finished 2nd with a reworked Red-Green list.

All eight decklists are already on the Tournament Decks page. Open any entry and click "Load into Deck Builder" to see the full 50-card list.

Final Standings

  • 1st Place — Gabriel, Yellow / Green — open deck
  • 2nd Place — Adzuf, Red / Green — open deck
  • Top 4 — Zul, Red / Blue — open deck
  • Top 4 — Andrei, Red / Blue — open deck
  • Top 8 — Ciko, Red / Blue — open deck
  • Top 8 — Prabu, Red / Blue — open deck
  • Top 8 — Ditya, Red / Yellow — open deck
  • Top 8 — Fahry, Red / Blue — open deck

Red in 7 of 8 Decks, but Not in the Winner's

[Covert Ops] Black Widow[Antimatter] Iron Man[Spider-Archenemy] Red Goblin

In September, all eight Top 8 decks played Red. This month seven did, and five of them were Red-Blue. Every one of those seven Red lists ran three copies of BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow, two or three of BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man and at least one SP01-006 [Spider-Archenemy] Red Goblin. Gabriel's winning list played none of them.

The Champion: Yellow-Green Web

[The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man[Vigil of Love] Amazing Spider-Man[Alliance Leader] Spider-Man 2099[Echo of Fate] Spider-Man

Gabriel's deck is 29 Yellow and 21 Green, and 37 of its 50 cards come from SP01. It is close to the Yellow-Green list that won the Asia Peak Invitational in Macao on October 11. Both run full playsets of the Yellow Web package: SP01-021 [The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man, SP01-022 [Phantom Strike] Ghost-Spider, SP01-025 [Gunfire Negotiation] Spider-Man Noir, SP01-026 [Vigil of Love] Amazing Spider-Man, SP01-027 [Symbiotic Suit] Spider-Man, SP01-030 [Invitation of Multiverse] Spider-Ham and SP01-032 [Alliance Leader] Spider-Man 2099. Both also play three copies each of SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-065 [Echo of Fate] Spider-Man and SP01-080 [Spider-Archenemy] Shocker on the Green side.

Compared with the Macao list, Gabriel:

  • Left out: SP01-034 [Ultimate Mode] Spider Suit, SP01-070 [Sense of Multiverse] Silk and SP01-077 [Reversal of Multiverse] Spider-Punk
  • Played fewer copies of: SP01-067 [Spider-Ally] Anti-Venom (1), SP01-068 [The Legacy] Superior Spider-Man (2), SP01-079 [Spider-Companion] Gwen Stacy (2) and SD04-002 [Clustered Arrow] Hawkeye (2), all 3-ofs in Macao
  • Added: 3× SD02-004 [Tough Guardian] Hulk, 3× SD02-012 [Hero File] Thor, 3× BP01-100 [Hearing Disarm] Daredevil, 2× SD04-013 [Hero File] Hawkeye and 2× SP01-029 [Spider-Mech] SP//dr

Adzuf: Back in the Final with a New Red-Green

Adzuf won September with Red-Green and reached the final again in the same colours, now 21 Red and 29 Green. Era of Spiders arrived in Indonesia after the September event, and his list went from no SP01 cards to 14: SP01-006 [Spider-Archenemy] Red Goblin, SP01-080 [Spider-Archenemy] Shocker, SP01-007 [Spider-Companion] Spinneret, SP01-076 [Spider-Archenemy] Morbius, SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-008 [Spider-Companion] Silver Sablinova and SP01-071 [Spider-Archenemy] Venom 2099.

The core is unchanged from September: 3× BP01-001 [Antimatter] Iron Man, 3× BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow, 3× BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor, 3× SD04-001 [God of Trick] Loki, 3× BP01-100 [Hearing Disarm] Daredevil and 3× BP01-106 [Search For Comrade] Falcon. Out went three copies each of BP01-097 [Original Mutant] Namor, BP01-029 [Cover Operations] War Machine and SD04-012 [Hero File] Ant-Man, two each of BP01-012 [Thunder Charge] Thor, SD04-003 [Quantum Rewind] Ant-Man and BP01-094 [God Of Story] Loki, and a few single copies.

Red-Blue: Two Ways to Build It

All five Red-Blue lists shared the same Captain America core: three copies each of BP01-077 [I Need You] Captain America, BP01-083 [Freedom Watch] New Captain America, BP01-064 [Quantum Core] Ant-Man and BP01-066 [Turn The Tide] Captain America, plus SD03-011 [Ancestral Plane] New Black Panther.

  • Knull Symbiotes (Andrei, Prabu): 40 Blue and 10 Red, built around 3× SP01-047 [God of Symbiotes] Knull with SP01-050 [Spider-Archenemy] Phage, SP01-051 [Spider-Archenemy] Agony, SP01-053 [Spider-Archenemy] Riot and SP01-054 [Spider-Archenemy] Lasher. The two lists are nearly identical: Prabu cut one BP01-067 [Hostile Focus] Winter Soldier, one SP01-042 [Spider-Archenemy] Green Goblin and one SP01-053 [Spider-Archenemy] Riot for one BP01-061 [Follow Shadow] New Black Panther and two BP01-063 [Time Abduction] Loki.
  • Avengers Red-Blue (Zul, Ciko, Fahry): fewer Spiders (6 to 11 SP01 cards) and more Red power, with 3× SD03-004 [Cold Shadow] New Black Panther in all three lists plus BP01-015 [Disintegration Ray] Vision and BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor. Zul, 2nd in September, made the Top 4 again with 9 SP01 cards, up from none last month.

Ditya: The Only Red-Yellow

[Hero File] Thor[Hero File] Vision[Hero File] Hulk[Red Room Legacy] Black Widow

Ditya's 26 Red / 24 Yellow list was the only Red-Yellow deck in the Top 8. It runs three copies each of SD02-012 [Hero File] Thor, SD02-013 [Hero File] Vision and SD01-013 [Hero File] Hulk, along with BP01-042 [Red Room Legacy] Black Widow, SD02-004 [Tough Guardian] Hulk and BP01-015 [Disintegration Ray] Vision.
