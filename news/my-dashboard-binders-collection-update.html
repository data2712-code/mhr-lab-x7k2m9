My Dashboard Update: Card Binders, Collection by Set & Version, and a Faster My Decks

23 Oktober 2026 · 8 menit baca · oleh Tim MHR Deck Lab

My Dashboard now has three tabs: My Decks, Card Collection and the new Binders. Record every version of your cards, sort your collection set by set, build 3×3 binders (even with photos of cards that aren't listed yet), and share them with a link or as an Instagram story to sell or trade cards.

My Dashboard just got its biggest update so far. It now has three tabs: My Decks, Card Collection and the brand-new Binders. You can record every version of a card you own, sort your collection set by set, build your own 3×3 card binders (even with photos of cards that aren't in our database yet), and share a binder with a link or as an image, for example to sell or trade cards.

Everything below is live now and free with an MHR Deck Lab account. We rolled it out in three steps.

At a Glance

  • Step A, a cleaner dashboard: tabs at the top, deck cards instead of a long list, and a Card Collection you can browse set by set, version by version.
  • Step B, Binders: 3×3 binder pages for your collection, your wishlist, or the cards you want to sell or trade, shared with a link. Cards that aren't in our database yet can be added with a photo.
  • Step C, save as image: turn any binder page into an Instagram / TikTok Story or Post image in one tap.
  • Also new: every account can now create its own event on the Events page.

Step A: My Decks

The three boxes at the top of My Dashboard are now its tabs, so you only see one part at a time. Your decks are no longer one long list:

  • Deck cards show cover art, the deck's colours, the card count (in red if it isn't 50 yet) and when you last changed it.
  • Search by deck name or by any card inside the deck. Type "Thunder Speed" and you see every deck that runs it.
  • Filter by colour, by public decks, or by decks that aren't 50 cards yet, and sort by last updated, name or oldest.
  • ★ Pin your main decks so they always stay at the top. The first 8 decks show right away; press Show all for the rest, or switch to the compact ☰ list view.
  • Open in Builder is one tap. Publish, version history, compare with your active deck, preview and delete are in the ⋯ menu.

Step A: Card Collection, set by set

Your collection is now split by set. Pick a set at the top (Starter Decks, Expansions, or Promo & other) and see your progress straight away: how many cards you own, how many are still missing, and how many versions you have.

Copy missing list puts every card you still need from that set on your clipboard, ready to paste into a WhatsApp group. New filters show only the cards you own, the ones you're missing, or cards where you're still missing a version.

Step A: Every version counts

A card like Odin «Guardian of the Nine Realms» (SD05-001) comes in UR, MR and SEC. Until now your collection only knew "3 Odins". Now every card with more than one print has a Versions button, so you can record exactly how many UR, MR, SEC or HR copies you have.

Your old collection is safe: everything you recorded before counts as the main version. The Deck Builder still adds all versions together, so "Missing cards" works exactly as before.

Step B: Binders

The new 📒 Binders tab works like a real 3×3 binder: 9 pockets per page, up to 50 pages, and up to 20 binders per account. Each binder has a type:

  • 🗂 My collection: show off a master set or your favourite cards.
  • 🏷 For sale / trade: the cards you want to sell or swap, with prices.
  • ⭐ Wishlist: the cards you're hunting for.

You can start with empty pages or with every card of a set in order (great for a master set).

Press ✏️ Edit and tap any pocket to choose a card, its version and how many copies. Move cards around with ◀ ▶, or drag them on a computer. Everything saves to your account automatically.

Step B: Selling and trading your cards

In a For sale / trade binder, every pocket can have:

  • a price in rupiah (the dots are added as you type, so 150000 becomes 150.000), with NEGO if you're open to offers (it's off by default, so you decide), or just "Make offer" if you'd rather not set a price,
  • the card's condition (Near mint, Lightly played, Played, Damaged) and a short note,
  • a Sold stamp once it's gone.

In ⚙ Settings you can add your WhatsApp number and Instagram, your city, and a note for buyers (for example "COD at Weekly Rush on Saturdays"). Turn on the shared link and send it anywhere. Buyers see your binder, a 💬 Chat on WhatsApp button with a ready-made message, and your Instagram. You can also hide your prices.

A few things work differently on purpose, to keep buyers and sellers safe:

  • Link-only. There's no public list of binders. Only people you send the link to can open it, and you can turn the link off at any time.
  • We're not part of the deal. Every shared sale or trade binder shows this notice: MHR Deck Lab is only a place to record collections and promote cards that players want to sell. We are not part of, and are not responsible for, any deal or transaction between users. Prices, payment, shipping and card condition are agreed between buyer and seller. Check the card in person or by video call before you pay, and be careful with payment requests that don't match the seller.
  • Report button. If a binder looks like a scam, fake cards, or anything inappropriate, press 🚩 Report this binder (you need to be logged in). Our team reviews every report, and a binder is hidden automatically after reports from 3 different accounts until we've checked it.

Step B: Card Not Listed Yet? Add a Photo

Some cards aren't in the MHR Deck Lab database yet, for example local event promos or cards we haven't added. You can now put them in a binder with a photo:

  • In ✏️ Edit mode, tap a pocket and choose 📷 Card not listed? Upload a photo.
  • Take a photo or pick one from your gallery. Any normal phone photo works: MHR Deck Lab crops it to the card shape and makes it smaller on your phone before uploading, so you don't need to convert or resize anything first.
  • Drag and zoom so the card fills the frame, then fill in the card name, rarity, set and quantity. In a For sale / trade binder you can also add the price, condition, a note, open to offers and sold.
  • Each account can upload up to 20 photos.
  • When you mark a photo card as Sold, its photo is deleted for good to free up space. The card name, price and other details stay in your binder.

Please only upload photos of your own cards. Photo cards show a 📷 badge, and visitors can see that the details were added by the binder owner, not checked by MHR Deck Lab.

Step C: Save your binder as an image

Want to post your binder on Instagram or TikTok? Press 🖼 Save pages as image and choose:

  • Story 9:16 (1080×1920) or Post 4:5 (1080×1350), one image per binder page,
  • all pages with cards, or just the page you're looking at,
  • whether to show prices and whether to leave out sold cards,
  • a QR code that opens your binder link.

The images show each card with its version, price, NEGO and SOLD stamps, your WhatsApp / Instagram for sale binders, and your binder link. Download them, or on your phone tap 📤 Share to Instagram / WhatsApp… to post them right away.

Also New: Create Your Own Event

The Events page is now open to everyone: any MHR Deck Lab account can create an event and run it with Swiss pairings, top cut and live standings. To keep things tidy, an account can have up to 10 events that aren't finished yet. Sending Top-cut decks to Tournament Decks is still only for verified organizers. If you run events at your store and want to be verified, message us on Instagram or TikTok @deteprtm.

How to Start

  1. Log in at mhrdecklab.com (free account).
  2. Open My Dashboard → Card Collection, pick a set and record your cards, including versions.
  3. Go to Binders → New binder, choose a type and how to start, then share the link or save the pages as images.
