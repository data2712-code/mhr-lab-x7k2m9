Asia Peak Invitational: Yellow-Green Wins in Macao, Yogie Takes 2nd

13 Oktober 2026 · 4 menit baca · oleh MHR Deck Lab Team

A near-pure Era of Spiders Yellow-Green list won the Marvel Hero Rush Asia Peak Invitational at ASCC Macao on October 11. Indonesia's Yogie finished 2nd and Radian made the Top 4. All four decklists inside.

The Marvel Hero Rush Asia Peak Invitational was played on October 11, 2026, as part of the 4th Asia Super Collectors Convention (ASCC) at The Venetian Macao Cotai Expo. Four Indonesian Qualifier winners made the trip, and two of them reached the Top 4: Yogie finished 2nd and Radian made the Top 4. The title went to a Yellow-Green Spider deck.

All four decklists are already on the Tournament Decks page. Open any entry and click "Load into Deck Builder" to see the full 50-card list.

Final Standings

  • 1st Place — 剩饭君 (Shengfanjun), Yellow / Green — open deck
  • 2nd Place — Yogie (Indonesia), Red / Green — open deck
  • Top 4 — Radian (Indonesia), Red / Blue — open deck
  • Top 4 — 落雨啦 (Luoyula), Blue / Green — open deck

Every Top 4 list ran a different colour pair, and Green showed up in three of them. Only Radian's Red-Blue list skipped it.

The Champion: Almost Pure Era of Spiders

[The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man[Spider-Companion] Black Cat[Echo of Fate] Spider-Man[The Legacy] Superior Spider-Man[Clustered Arrow] Hawkeye

Shengfanjun's winning list is 23 Yellow and 27 Green, and 47 of its 50 cards come from SP01. The only cards from outside Era of Spiders are three copies of SD04-002 [Clustered Arrow] Hawkeye. Sixteen of the seventeen slots are full playsets; SP01-034 [Ultimate Mode] Spider Suit is the lone 2-of.

The Yellow side is the Web package: SP01-021 [The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man, SP01-022 [Phantom Strike] Ghost-Spider, SP01-030 [Invitation of Multiverse] Spider-Ham, SP01-025 [Gunfire Negotiation] Spider-Man Noir, SP01-032 [Alliance Leader] Spider-Man 2099, SP01-026 [Vigil of Love] Amazing Spider-Man and SP01-027 [Symbiotic Suit] Spider-Man. SP01-021 [The Web of Destiny] Spider-Man lets you attach a Web character from HAND to a character on the field whenever one of your Web cards enters, and can be placed back into your BASE when your effect sends it to VOID. The Green side adds SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-067 [Spider-Ally] Anti-Venom, SP01-070 [Sense of Multiverse] Silk, SP01-077 [Reversal of Multiverse] Spider-Punk, SP01-079 [Spider-Companion] Gwen Stacy, SP01-080 [Spider-Archenemy] Shocker, SP01-068 [The Legacy] Superior Spider-Man and SP01-065 [Echo of Fate] Spider-Man.

Yogie: 2nd Place with Red-Green

Yogie, who won the Batam Qualifier on October 4, brought almost the same Red-Green list to Macao: 17 Red and 33 Green, with 18 SP01 cards. He swapped five cards:

  • Out: 2× BP01-013 [Wakanda Forever] Black Panther, 2× BP01-022 [Top Agent] Black Widow, 1× BP01-104 [Time Reversal] Scarlet Witch (down from 2 copies to 1)
  • In: 2× SP01-067 [Spider-Ally] Anti-Venom (up from 1 copy to 3), 2× BP01-015 [Disintegration Ray] Vision, 1× SP01-006 [Spider-Archenemy] Red Goblin (up from 2 copies to 3)

The core stayed the same: full playsets of SD04-001 [God of Trick] Loki, BP01-100 [Hearing Disarm] Daredevil, BP01-106 [Search For Comrade] Falcon, SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-071 [Spider-Archenemy] Venom 2099, BP01-095 [Sacrifice For Justice] Captain America, BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow and BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor.

Radian: Top 4 with Spider-Heavy Red-Blue

Radian, the Bandung Qualifier winner, kept his Spider-Archenemy Red-Blue build (16 Red, 34 Blue, 38 SP01 cards) and changed only two cards from Bandung: one SP01-009 [Spider-Ally] Black Panther and one SP01-049 [Spider-Ally] Harry Osborn came out, and single copies of BP01-061 [Follow Shadow] New Black Panther and BP01-066 [Turn The Tide] Captain America went in.

The list is built around SP01-047 [God of Symbiotes] Knull with full playsets of SP01-042 [Spider-Archenemy] Green Goblin, SP01-043 [Spider-Archenemy] Kingpin, SP01-050 [Spider-Archenemy] Phage, SP01-051 [Spider-Archenemy] Agony, SP01-053 [Spider-Archenemy] Riot, SP01-054 [Spider-Archenemy] Lasher and SP01-058 [Spider-Archenemy] Tombstone, plus SP01-001 [Spider-Archenemy] Carnage as the Red finisher.

Luoyula: Top 4 with Blue-Green Avengers

[Supreme Watcher] Captain America[God of Trick] Loki[Restoration of Multiverse] Doctor Strange[I Need You] Captain America

落雨啦 (Luoyula) played the least SP01 of the four lists, only 10 cards, in a Blue-Green Avengers build: 24 Blue and 26 Green. It runs SD03-001 [Supreme Watcher] Captain America and SD04-001 [God of Trick] Loki at 3 copies each, plus SP01-066 [Restoration of Multiverse] Doctor Strange, PB01-011 [Astral Visitor] Doctor Strange, BP01-077 [I Need You] Captain America, BP01-090 [Freedom Decision] Captain America and BP01-085 [Void Banish] Scarlet Witch, with a single EB01-008 [Chaos Origins] Scarlet Witch and SP01-062 [King of Hell] Mephisto.

Cards That Crossed Over

No card appeared in all four lists. Twelve cards made exactly two, and all of them were played as full playsets except BP01-104 [Time Reversal] Scarlet Witch (1 and 2 copies):

  • Shengfanjun and Yogie: SP01-063 [Spider-Companion] Black Cat, SP01-067 [Spider-Ally] Anti-Venom, SP01-080 [Spider-Archenemy] Shocker
  • Yogie and Radian: BP01-002 [Covert Ops] Black Widow, BP01-011 [Thunder Speed] Thor, SP01-006 [Spider-Archenemy] Red Goblin
  • Yogie and Luoyula: BP01-100 [Hearing Disarm] Daredevil, BP01-106 [Search For Comrade] Falcon, SD04-001 [God of Trick] Loki, BP01-104 [Time Reversal] Scarlet Witch
  • Radian and Luoyula: SP01-042 [Spider-Archenemy] Green Goblin, SP01-058 [Spider-Archenemy] Tombstone
