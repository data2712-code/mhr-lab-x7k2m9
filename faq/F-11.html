Rules FAQ · CALL, BASE and Face-Down Cards

F-11 · If I flip a Face-Down Card face-up, did it "enter the field"?

No. Flipping a Face-Down Card face-up is not placing it onto the FIELD, so "when this card enters the field" effects do not trigger. The flipped Character can still use BATTLE-BASE MOVE that turn.

You cannot flip a Face-Down Card just because you want to. No game action lets you do that. A Face-Down Card is turned face-up only when an effect tells you to flip it. Flipping your own Face-Down Card without such an effect is not legal.

Example: SP01-061 「Spider-Archenemy」 Mr. Negative. Its ACTI【HAND】 can be used on your turn if you have not called a Character and you have as many set cards (Face-Down Cards) as Characters in your BASE. You place Mr. Negative from your HAND onto your WING, and then "you flip all your cards in BASE". This flips every card in your BASE: your Characters in BASE are turned face-down first, then your Face-Down Cards are flipped face-up. The cards that were flipped face-up because of this effect:

  • they did not enter the field, so their "when this card enters the field" effects do not trigger;
  • they can still use BATTLE-BASE MOVE this turn;
  • Mr. Negative itself was placed onto the FIELD, so its own TRIG ("when this card enters the field") does trigger.

Without an effect like this, your Face-Down Cards stay face-down.

Source: CR 301.2.a.2, 301.22.a–c. Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia, 1 Oct 2026.
