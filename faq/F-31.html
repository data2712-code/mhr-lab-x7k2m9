Rules FAQ · Effects and timing

F-31 · I drew a card while effects were resolving. Can its HAND trigger still be used?

Yes. If your HAND changes while effects of one timing are resolving, and the new card has a trigger for that same timing, it counts as triggered at that timing.

Example:

  1. Your opponent has SD03-011 「Ancestral Plane」 New Black Panther in a WING. Its AUTO gives your Character in FRONT Power -500.
  2. You call BP01-077 「I Need You」 Captain America into your FRONT. It enters the field, and at the same moment its Power decreases because of Ancestral Plane.
  3. I Need You's trigger resolves: you draw 1 card and it is BP01-063 「Time Abduction」 Loki ("when the Power of your character decreases"). Then you place the top card of your Deck face-down into your BASE.
  4. The Power decrease happened at the same timing that is still being resolved, so Time Abduction counts as triggered. You may prune it from your HAND and use its effect.
Source: CR 304.1.k. Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia, 21 Oct 2026.
