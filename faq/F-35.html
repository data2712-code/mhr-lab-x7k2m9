Rules FAQ · Power, R and leaving the field

F-35 · What counts as "Power decreased" (for cards like 「Time Abduction」 Loki)?

Any effect that gives a Character Power -X counts, including an effect from your own card. A buff that ends is not a decrease.

Source: Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia, 29 Aug, 24 Sep and 6 Oct 2026.
