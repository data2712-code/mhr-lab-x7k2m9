F-44 · What is the difference between a "character" and a "card" in card text?

They are not the same, so read the card text carefully.

  • Card means any card: a Character, a Face-Down Card or an Attached Card.
  • Character means only a face-up Character. Every card in your Deck is a Character, but on the FIELD it can stop being a Character:
    • a Face-Down Card in BASE is a card, not a Character (it becomes a Character again when flipped face-up);
    • an Attached Card is a card, not a Character (it becomes a Character again when detached).
  • So an effect that says "1 of your characters" cannot choose a Face-Down Card or an Attached Card, while an effect that says "1 of your cards" can.

Examples:

  • BP01-061 「Follow Shadow」 New Black Panther: "retreat 2 of your cards in BASE" → face-up Characters and Face-Down Cards both count.
  • SP01-026 「Vigil of Love」 Amazing Spider-Man: "swap this card in HAND and 1 of your card with 【Ghost-Spider】 or 【Gwen】…" → it can also swap with a Gwen that is an Attached Card.
  • A CALL cost retreats Characters (Face-Down Cards are allowed by a special rule and count as Lv1), so an Attached Card can never be part of a CALL cost.
Source: CR 201.1–201.2, 301.21.a–b, 301.21.g, 301.22.a, 301.25.a, 301.26.a, 301.19.b.
