F-03 · How does the game start? Can I redraw my starting hand (mulligan)?

  • Each player draws 6 cards.
  • Each player may mulligan once. The first player decides first, then the second player.
  • Choose how many of your 6 cards you want to replace: from 1 up to all 6. You can also keep your hand as it is.
  • Put the cards you are replacing on the bottom of your Deck face-down, then draw the same number from the top so you have 6 cards again. Then shuffle your Deck.
  • Example: you replace 2 cards. Put those 2 on the bottom of your Deck, draw 2 from the top, then shuffle.

Try it with the Opening Draw Simulation in the Deck Builder.

Source: CR 303.1.d–e.
