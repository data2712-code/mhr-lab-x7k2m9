Rules FAQ · Counter Step and Counter Phase

F-23 · How does the Counter Step work? Can we counter more than once?

  • In each battle's Counter Step, players alternate one action at a time, starting with the opponent of the turn player.
  • An action is: a COUNTER CALL, activating a COUNTER·ACTI effect, or passing.
  • You can act again on your next turn to act. The step ends only when both players pass in a row.
  • The Counter Phase (after the Battle Phase) works the same way.

Example of one battle (Player A is the turn player; counter = a COUNTER CALL or activating a COUNTER·ACTI effect, no counter = pass):

  1. Player A attacks.
  2. Player B: no counter.
  3. Player A: counter.
  4. Player B: counter.
  5. Player A: counter.
  6. Player B: no counter.
  7. Player A: no counter.
  8. Both players passed in a row (6 and 7), so the Counter Step ends and the battle is resolved.

Player B's pass in step 2 did not end the step, because Player A did not pass right after it. Remember that each player can make only one COUNTER CALL in the Battle Phase (F-24), so Player A's two counters here cannot both be COUNTER CALLs.

Source: CR 303.2.a.4.3.2.1–303.2.a.4.3.2.4, 303.2.a.5.1–303.2.a.5.3. This replaced the older "one action each" rule; announced by the Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia on 24 Sep 2026.
