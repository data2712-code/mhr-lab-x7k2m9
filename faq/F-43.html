F-43 · Can 「Turn The Tide」 Captain America return a Character that retreated because its Power became 0?

Yes. If an opponent's effect reduces your Lv4 or lower (Human) Character's Power to 0, the Character retreats by the game rule, and this counts as entering the RETREAT from BATTLE by your opponent's effect. You may use BP01-066 / EB01-012 「Turn The Tide」 Captain America.

Source: CR 301.16.a.1, 301.16.b. Confirmed by the Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia (Nov 2026).
