Rules FAQ · CALL, BASE and Face-Down Cards

F-09 · Is "placed" the same as "called"? Does a "when called" effect trigger if a card is placed by an effect?

No. CALL is the rule action in CR 301.19: it places a Character from your HAND by paying its cost. An effect that says "place" puts a card onto the FIELD without a CALL. So effects that say "when this card enters the field by calling" do not trigger when the card is placed by an effect (for example by 「Turn The Tide」 Captain America).

Source: CR 301.12.a, 301.19. Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia, 14 and 29 Aug 2026.
