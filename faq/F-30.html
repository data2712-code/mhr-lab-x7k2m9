Rules FAQ · Effects and timing

F-30 · Can I use an effect if its result has no valid target?

Yes, if the first part can be done. The first part (for example discarding or pruning the card) happens and the result does nothing. Examples:

  • Discarding SD01-006 「Hourglass Bite」 Black Widow from your HAND when the opponent has no matching Character.
  • SP01-006 「Spider-Archenemy」 Red Goblin: you prune Red Goblin and 1 other Character or Attached Card on your field, and X is the total Lv of the cards you pruned. You may do this even if your opponent has no Character with exactly LvX in BATTLE. Your two cards are pruned, and nothing happens to your opponent.
Source: Head Judge of Marvel Hero Rush Indonesia, 25 Oct 2026 (he noted he might be wrong; this will be updated if an official ruling says otherwise).
