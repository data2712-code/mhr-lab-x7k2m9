Rules FAQ · Counter Step and Counter Phase

F-26 · When can I use ACTI and COUNTER·ACTI effects?

  • ACTI: only in your own Action Phase.
  • COUNTER·ACTI: in your own Action Phase, in either player's Counter Phase, and in the Counter Step.
Source: CR 201.10.f.2–201.10.f.3.
